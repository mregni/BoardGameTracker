The app is available in English, Dutch (Belgium), Dutch (Netherlands) and Spanish. Translations are managed on Crowdin.

<LinkButton href="https://crowdin.com/project/boardgametracker" icon="external">
	Help translate on Crowdin
</LinkButton>

## How to contribute

Open the BoardGameTracker project on Crowdin and follow its instructions to suggest translations. To add a language that is not listed yet, ask on Crowdin or open a feature request on GitHub.
